<objective>
Build the navigable wizard as a thin vertical slice: a "use client" Wizard owning currentStep
and WizardState, five step components (project type, platform, modules, details, completion stub),
a navigation/progress bar, the Sonner Toaster mounted in layout, and page.tsx rewired to render
the wizard. A failing end-to-end navigation test is written first.

get blocked (with a toast) when a required choice is missing, go back without losing state, see
the step counter update (3 or 4), and reach the Готово! completion stub.

preserved choices (WIZ-01 … WIZ-09, UI-03).
